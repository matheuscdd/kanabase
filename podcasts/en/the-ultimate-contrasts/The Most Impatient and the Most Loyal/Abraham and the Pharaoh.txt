# Abraham and the Pharaoh

During Abraham’s journey, a time of famine came upon the land.

There was very little food, so Abraham and Sarah traveled to Egypt to survive.

As they neared Egypt, Abraham became afraid.

Sarah was very beautiful, and Abraham worried that the Egyptians might hurt him because she was his wife.

So Abraham said to Sarah,
“Please say that you are my sister, so the people will treat me well.”

When they arrived in Egypt, the leaders of Pharaoh’s palace noticed Sarah’s beauty.

Soon, Sarah was taken into Pharaoh’s house.

Pharaoh treated Abraham kindly because he believed Sarah was only Abraham’s sister.

He gave Abraham animals, servants, and many gifts.

But God knew the truth.

And because of the lie, trouble came upon Pharaoh’s house.

Pharaoh realized something was wrong.

Then he learned the truth.

Sarah was not just Abraham’s sister — she was his wife.

Pharaoh called Abraham and said,
“Why did you not tell me the truth?”

Abraham felt ashamed.

Pharaoh returned Sarah to Abraham and sent them safely away from Egypt.

Even though Abraham made a wrong choice because of fear, God still protected him and Sarah.

This story reminds us that fear can lead people to make bad decisions.

But God remains faithful, even when people fail.
