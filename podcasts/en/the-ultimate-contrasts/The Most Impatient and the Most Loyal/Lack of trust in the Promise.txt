Lack of trust in the Promise

God had promised Abraham that one day he would have a great family.

But many years passed…
And Abraham and his wife Sarah still did not have a child.

They began to wonder how God’s promise would happen.

Sarah had a servant named Hagar.

One day, Sarah said to Abraham,
“Maybe God’s promise will come through Hagar.”

So Hagar became part of Abraham’s family.

Soon, Hagar was going to have a baby.

But as time passed, trouble began in the home.

Hagar became proud, and Sarah became hurt and upset.
The two women argued and became unhappy with each other.

One day, Hagar became so sad that she ran away into the desert.

The hot sun burned above her, and she felt alone and afraid.

But God saw Hagar.

An angel of the Lord came to her and spoke kindly.

“Hagar, do not be afraid,” the angel said.
“God sees you and cares for you.”

Then the angel gave her good news.

“You will have a son, and his name will be Ishmael.”

The name Ishmael means “God hears.”

God had heard Hagar’s cries in the desert.

So Hagar returned home.

And soon, she gave birth to a baby boy.

Abraham named him Ishmael, just as God had said.

This story reminds us that God sees people when they feel forgotten or alone.

Even in the desert…
Even in difficult moments…
God hears, and God cares.

The end.
