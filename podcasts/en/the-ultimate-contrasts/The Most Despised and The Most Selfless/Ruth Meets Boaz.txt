Ruth Meets Boaz

After Naomi and Ruth arrived in Bethlehem, they were very poor.

They had no fields, no husband to provide for them, and very little food.

So one morning, Ruth said to Naomi,

“Let me go into the fields and gather leftover grain behind the workers.”

Naomi nodded gently.

“Go, my daughter.”

In those days, workers harvesting grain would leave small pieces behind for the poor to collect.

So Ruth walked into the fields under the warm sun and began gathering grain one piece at a time.

Now the field belonged to a man named Boaz.

Boaz was wealthy, respected, and a relative of Naomi’s husband.

That day, Boaz arrived to check on his workers.

As he looked across the field, he noticed Ruth working quietly and diligently.

“Who is that young woman?” Boaz asked.

The workers answered,

“She is Ruth, the Moabite woman who came back with Naomi from Moab.”

Boaz approached Ruth kindly.

“My daughter, stay in my fields,” he said.
“Do not go anywhere else. Stay close to my workers, and no one will bother you.”

Ruth was surprised by his kindness.

She bowed respectfully and asked,

“Why are you being so kind to me? I am a foreigner.”

But Boaz answered,

“I have heard about everything you have done for Naomi — how you left your home and came to a land you did not know.”

Then Boaz blessed her.

“May the Lord reward you for your kindness.”

At mealtime, Boaz invited Ruth to eat with the workers.

He even told his servants to leave extra grain behind for her on purpose.

By evening, Ruth had gathered far more food than expected.

When she returned home, Naomi was amazed.

“Where did you gather all this grain?” Naomi asked.

Then Ruth told her about Boaz.

As soon as Naomi heard his name, hope began to grow in her heart.

“Boaz is one of our family redeemers,” Naomi said.

And so Ruth continued working in Boaz’s fields during the harvest season.

What seemed like a simple day in the fields…

Was actually the beginning of something much greater.
