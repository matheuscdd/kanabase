Ruth Visits Boaz

As the harvest season continued, Naomi began thinking about Ruth’s future.

She wanted Ruth to have a safe home and a good husband.

One evening, Naomi said,

“My daughter, I must help you find security for your future.”

Then Naomi spoke about Boaz.

“He is our relative, and tonight he will be at the threshing floor, separating the grain.”

Naomi gave Ruth careful instructions.

“Wash yourself, put on your best clothes, and go to the threshing floor.
But wait quietly until Boaz has finished eating and resting.”

Ruth listened closely.

“I will do everything you say,” she answered.

That night, Ruth went quietly to the threshing floor.

The cool night air moved softly through the fields.

After Boaz finished eating, he lay down near the piles of grain and fell asleep.

Carefully and respectfully, Ruth approached him and lay down near his feet.

In the middle of the night, Boaz suddenly woke up.

Startled, he looked down and saw someone there.

“Who are you?” he asked.

“I am Ruth,” she answered softly.
“Please spread your covering over me, because you are a family redeemer.”

Boaz understood what Ruth meant.

She was asking him to protect and care for her by marrying her.

Boaz was deeply moved by her kindness and humility.

“May the Lord bless you, my daughter,” he said.
“You have shown great kindness.”

But Boaz explained that there was another relative closer to Naomi’s family than he was.

“If he wishes to redeem you, let him do so,” Boaz said.
“But if not, I will do it.”

Before sunrise, Boaz filled Ruth’s shawl with barley grain so she would not return to Naomi empty-handed.

Then Ruth went back home.

Naomi eagerly asked,

“What happened, my daughter?”

Ruth told her everything Boaz had said and done.

Naomi smiled with hope.

“Wait patiently,” she said.
“Boaz will not rest until this matter is settled today.”

And so Ruth waited…

Not knowing that her life was about to change forever.
