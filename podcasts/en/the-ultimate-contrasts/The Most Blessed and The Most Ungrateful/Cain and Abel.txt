Cain and Abel

After Adam and Eve left the Garden of Eden, they started a family.

Soon, they had two sons.

The older brother was named Cain.
Cain worked in the fields, growing fruits and vegetables from the ground.

The younger brother was named Abel.
Abel was a shepherd who cared for sheep and lambs.

One day, the two brothers brought offerings to God.

Cain brought some food from his fields.
Abel brought the very best lamb from his flock.

God was pleased with Abel’s offering because Abel gave with love and faith.

But Cain became angry and jealous.

His face grew sad and hard.

God spoke kindly to Cain.

“Why are you angry?” God asked.
“If you do what is right, everything will be well.”

But Cain did not listen.

One day, Cain asked Abel to walk with him into the field.

Out there, far from everyone else, Cain attacked his brother Abel.

And Abel died.

It was the first terrible act of violence in the world.

Later, God called to Cain.

“Where is your brother Abel?”

Cain answered,
“I don’t know. Am I supposed to watch over my brother?”

But God already knew the truth.

Cain had done something very wrong.

Because of his sin, Cain had to leave his home and wander far away.

Yet even then, God showed mercy to Cain and protected him.

This story teaches us something important:

Jealousy and anger can grow into terrible choices if we do not stop them.

But God wants people to choose love, kindness, and what is right.

And even when people fail…
God still shows mercy.

The end.
