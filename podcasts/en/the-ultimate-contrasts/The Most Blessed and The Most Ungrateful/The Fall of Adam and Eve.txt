The Fall of Adam and Eve

Long ago, in the beautiful Garden of Eden, everything was perfect.
The grass was green.
The rivers were clear.
Animals walked peacefully among the trees.

And in the middle of the garden lived two people: Adam and Eve.

God loved them very much.
He gave them delicious fruit to eat, beautiful places to explore, and every animal to care for.

But God gave them one important rule.

“You may eat fruit from any tree in the garden,” God said,
“except for the tree in the middle of the garden. Do not eat its fruit.”

Adam and Eve understood.

One day, a serpent came quietly to Eve.
The serpent was very sneaky.

“Did God really say you cannot eat from that tree?” the serpent asked.

Eve answered,
“We can eat from every tree except that one. God said if we eat it, we will die.”

But the serpent whispered,
“You will not die. Eat the fruit, and you will become wise.”

Eve looked at the fruit.
It looked beautiful and delicious.

Slowly… she reached out her hand.
She picked the fruit.
And she took a bite.

Then she gave some to Adam, and he ate it too.

At that moment, everything changed.

Adam and Eve suddenly felt afraid.
For the first time, they felt shame and sadness.

When they heard God walking in the garden, they hid behind the trees.

“Adam, where are you?” God called.

Adam answered softly,
“I was afraid, so I hid.”

God knew what had happened.

Because Adam and Eve disobeyed Him, sin entered the world.
Pain, sadness, and death came into creation.

Adam and Eve had to leave the beautiful garden.

But even then… God still loved them.

And He promised that one day, He would send a Savior to bring people back to Him.

So Adam and Eve left the garden…
But God’s story of love was only beginning.

The end.
