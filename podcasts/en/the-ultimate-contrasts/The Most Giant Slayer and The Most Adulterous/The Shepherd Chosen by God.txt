The Shepherd Chosen by God

After King Saul disobeyed God, the Lord told the prophet Samuel that a new king would one day replace him.

So God sent Samuel to the town of Bethlehem, to the house of a man named Jesse.

Samuel arrived carrying a horn filled with oil for anointing.

When the elders of the town saw the prophet coming, they trembled with fear.

“Do you come in peace?” they asked.

“Yes,” Samuel answered gently. “I have come to offer a sacrifice to the Lord.”

Then Samuel invited Jesse and his sons to join the gathering.

As the sons of Jesse stood before him, Samuel saw the oldest brother, Eliab. He was tall, strong, and looked like a king.

Surely this must be the chosen one, Samuel thought.

But God spoke to Samuel’s heart:

“Do not judge by appearance or height. People look at the outside, but the Lord looks at the heart.”

One by one, seven of Jesse’s sons passed before Samuel.

Yet God chose none of them.

Finally Samuel asked,

“Are these all your sons?”

Jesse hesitated.

“There is still the youngest,” he replied. “But he is out in the fields caring for the sheep.”

“Send for him,” Samuel said. “We will not continue until he arrives.”

Soon the youngest son came running from the hills.

His name was David.

He was only a shepherd boy, smelling of the fields and carrying the dust of the countryside.

But when Samuel saw him, the Lord said,

“Rise and anoint him. This is the one.”

In front of his brothers, Samuel poured oil over David’s head.

From that day forward, the Spirit of the Lord came powerfully upon him.

No crown rested on David’s head yet. No army followed him. No palace belonged to him.

To most people, he was still only a shepherd.

But God had already chosen him to become the future king of Israel.
