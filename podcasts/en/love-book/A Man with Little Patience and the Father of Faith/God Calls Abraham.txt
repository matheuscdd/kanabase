God Calls Abraham

Long ago, there was a man named Abraham.

Abraham lived far away in a land with his family.
He lived in tents, cared for animals, and traveled from place to place.

One day, God spoke to Abraham.

“Leave your home and go to the land that I will show you.”

This was a very big command.

God did not show Abraham a map.
He did not explain every step.

But God made a wonderful promise.

“I will bless you.
I will make your family into a great nation.
And through you, all the families of the earth will be blessed.”

Abraham listened carefully.

He trusted God, even though he did not know where the journey would lead.

So Abraham packed his belongings.
He gathered his servants and animals.
And together with his wife Sarah, he began the long journey.

Day after day, they traveled across deserts and hills.
At night, Abraham looked up at the countless stars shining in the sky.

And God spoke again.

“Look at the stars, Abraham.
One day, your descendants will be as many as the stars in the heavens.”

Abraham believed God.

Even when things seemed impossible…
Even when he had questions…
He continued to trust and obey.

And God stayed with Abraham wherever he went.

The story of Abraham teaches us that faith means trusting God, even when we cannot see the whole path ahead.

And sometimes, the greatest journeys begin with a single step of faith.

The end.
