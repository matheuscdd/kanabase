The Tower of Babel

After the flood, Noah’s family grew, and people began filling the earth again.

At that time, everyone in the world spoke the same language.
People could understand each other perfectly.

One day, a group of people traveled to a wide land called Babel.

There, they had a big idea.

“Let’s build a great city,” they said.
“And let’s build a tower so tall that it reaches the heavens!”

They wanted everyone to admire them.
They wanted to make themselves famous instead of honoring God.

So the people worked together.

They made bricks and carried them higher and higher.
The tower grew taller every day.

But God saw what they were doing.

The people were becoming proud.
They believed they did not need God.

So God decided to stop them.

Suddenly, something strange happened.

People began speaking different languages!

One worker shouted something…
But his friend could not understand him anymore.

Another person gave instructions…
But nobody knew what he meant.

Confusion spread everywhere.

People could no longer work together because they spoke different languages.

So the building of the tower stopped.

And the people scattered across the earth, forming many nations and languages.

That place became known as Babel, because there God confused the languages of the people.

The story reminds us that pride can lead people away from God.

But it also reminds us that God is greater than any tower people could ever build.

The end.
