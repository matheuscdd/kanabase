The Seven Days of Creation

In the beginning… there was nothing.
No light. No sky. No oceans. No animals.
Only darkness… and silence.

Then, God spoke.

“Let there be light.”

And suddenly, light burst into the darkness.
Day and night were separated.
The light was called Day… and the darkness was called Night.

This was the first day.

On the second day, God created the sky.
He stretched the heavens above the waters, creating the great expanse overhead.
Soft clouds began to drift across the endless blue.

On the third day, God gathered the waters together so dry land could appear.
Mountains rose. Valleys formed.
God called the dry land Earth, and the waters Seas.

Then, the earth began to bloom.
Grass covered the fields.
Trees grew tall and strong.
Flowers opened in every color imaginable.

And God saw that it was good.

On the fourth day, God placed lights in the sky.
The sun to rule the day…
The moon to shine at night…
And countless stars scattered across the heavens like diamonds.

On the fifth day, God filled the waters and skies with life.
Fish swam through the oceans.
Whales leaped through the waves.
Birds soared high above the earth, singing their beautiful songs.

On the sixth day, God created every creature that walks upon the land.
Lions roared.
Horses ran across the plains.
Tiny insects crawled through the grass.

Then, God created something special.

Human beings.

He created man and woman in His own image, giving them life, purpose, and a beautiful world to care for.

And God looked at everything He had made…
The oceans, the stars, the animals, and humanity…

And it was very good.

On the seventh day, God rested.
Not because He was tired…
But because creation was complete.

And peace filled the world.
