# God’s Promise to Abraham and Sarah

Many years had passed since God first promised Abraham a son.

Abraham and Sarah were now very old.

Sometimes, the promise seemed impossible.

One hot day, Abraham was sitting near his tent when he saw three visitors coming toward him.

Quickly, Abraham welcomed them with kindness.
He brought water for them to wash their feet and prepared a meal for them to eat.

As they rested, one of the visitors spoke.

“I will return next year,” he said,
“and Sarah will have a son.”

Sarah was listening from inside the tent.

When she heard those words, she quietly laughed to herself.

“How could I have a baby now?” she thought.
“I am too old.”

Then the Lord asked Abraham,
“Why did Sarah laugh?
Is anything too hard for the Lord?”

Sarah became afraid and said,
“I didn’t laugh.”

But the Lord gently answered,
“Yes, you did laugh.”

Still, God did not forget His promise.

And just as He had said, the miracle happened.

One year later, Sarah gave birth to a baby boy.

Abraham and Sarah were filled with joy.

They named the child Isaac.

The name Isaac means “laughter,” because Sarah had laughed when she first heard the promise.

Abraham held his son in his arms and thanked God.

After so many years of waiting…
God had kept His word.

The story of Isaac reminds us that God keeps His promises, even when things seem impossible.

Nothing is too hard for God.
