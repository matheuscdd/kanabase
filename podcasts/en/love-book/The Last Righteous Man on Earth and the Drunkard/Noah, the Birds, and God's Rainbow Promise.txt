Noah, the Birds, and God’s Rainbow Promise

Rain fell for many days and nights.

The waters covered the earth, even the tallest mountains.
But safely inside the ark, Noah, his family, and all the animals were protected.

Then, after a long time, the rain finally stopped.

Slowly… the waters began to go down.

One day, Noah opened a window on the ark.

First, he sent out a raven.

The bird flew back and forth over the waters, searching for dry land.

Later, Noah sent out a dove.

But the dove could not find a place to rest, so it returned to the ark.

Noah waited patiently.

Seven days later, he sent the dove out again.

This time, the dove returned carrying a fresh olive leaf in its beak.

Noah smiled.

Dry land was beginning to appear.

After waiting seven more days, Noah sent the dove out one last time.

And this time… the dove did not return.

The earth was dry again.

Then God spoke to Noah.

“Come out of the ark, you, your family, and all the animals.”

Slowly, the great door opened.

Animals hurried out into the fresh world.
Birds filled the sky once again.
The earth was alive with sound and movement.

Noah and his family stepped onto dry ground.

And the first thing Noah did was thank God.

Then God made a wonderful promise.

“I will never again destroy the whole earth with a flood.”

To remind the world of His promise, God placed a rainbow in the sky.

Bright colors stretched across the clouds.

And whenever people see a rainbow, they can remember God’s promise, His mercy, and His love.

The end.
