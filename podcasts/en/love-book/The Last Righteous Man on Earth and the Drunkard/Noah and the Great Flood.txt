Noah and the Great Flood

Many years passed after Cain and Abel.

More and more people filled the earth.
But something sad happened.

People became very cruel.

They lied, fought, hurt each other, and forgot about God.
Everywhere God looked, He saw violence and evil.

The world that had once been beautiful was now full of sin.

This made God very sad.

But there was one man who was different.

His name was Noah.

Noah loved God and tried to do what was right.
He was honest, kind, and faithful, even when everyone around him was doing evil things.

So God spoke to Noah.

“Noah,” God said,
“I am going to send a great flood to wash away the evil in the world. But I will save you and your family.”

Then God gave Noah a very strange command.

“Build a giant ark.”

An ark was a huge wooden boat.

God told Noah exactly how to build it.
It would be enormous, with many rooms inside.

Even though Noah had probably never seen a flood like this before…
He obeyed God.

Day after day, Noah worked on the ark.

People laughed at him.
They pointed and mocked him.

“Why are you building such a huge boat?” they asked.

But Noah continued to trust God.

When the ark was finally ready, God gave another command.

“Bring your family into the ark.
And bring animals too — two of every kind.”

Soon, animals began arriving from everywhere.

Big elephants stomped forward.
Tiny rabbits hopped along.
Birds fluttered through the sky.
Lions, sheep, horses, and countless other creatures entered the ark.

Then Noah, his wife, his sons, and their wives went inside.

And God closed the door.

Dark clouds filled the sky.

Rain began to fall.

And the great flood started.

The end.
