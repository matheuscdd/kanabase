The Giant and the Shepherd

Long ago, the armies of Israel and the Philistines stood facing each other on opposite hills, separated by a wide valley.

Every morning, a giant warrior named Goliath stepped forward from the Philistine camp.

He was enormous, covered in heavy armor, and carried a massive spear.

With a booming voice, he mocked Israel and shouted,

“Choose one man to fight me! If he defeats me, we will serve you. But if I win, you will serve us!”

The soldiers of Israel were terrified.

Even King Saul was afraid.

For forty days, Goliath came forward repeating the same challenge, and no one dared to face him.

At that time, a young shepherd named David arrived at the battlefield bringing food to his older brothers.

When David heard Goliath insulting Israel and mocking God, anger rose in his heart.

“Why is everyone afraid of him?” David asked. “The Lord who rescued me from lions and bears will rescue me from this giant too.”

King Saul heard about David and summoned him.

“You are only a boy,” Saul warned. “But Goliath has been a warrior since his youth.”

Still, David trusted God completely.

Saul finally agreed and offered David his royal armor, but it was too heavy and uncomfortable.

So David removed it.

Instead, he took only his shepherd’s staff, a sling, and five smooth stones from a nearby stream.

As David walked toward him, Goliath laughed loudly.

“Am I a dog that you come at me with sticks?” the giant mocked.

But David answered boldly,

“You come with sword and spear, but I come in the name of the Lord Almighty.”

Then David placed a stone into his sling and swung it with all his strength.

The stone flew through the air and struck Goliath directly in the forehead.

The giant staggered… then crashed to the ground.

Silence filled the valley for a moment.

Then the army of Israel erupted with victory cries as the Philistines fled in fear.

That day, a young shepherd defeated a giant — not by strength or armor, but by faith in God.
