There was a Lion in the Middle of the Road

[description] The boy Samson grew, and a restless, divine energy began to stir within him under the ancient oaks of Zorah. His hair fell in thick, heavy locks down his back, a wild and untouchable testament to the sacred vow made before he drew his first breath.

[conflict] Yet, as he reached the prime of his manhood, his eyes wandered dangerously far from his own people. Against the desperate pleas of his parents, Samson’s heart became utterly captivated by a Philistine woman from Timnah—a daughter of the very enemy he was prophesied to destroy.

[action] One blazing afternoon, while journeying alone through the rugged vineyards toward her village, a deafening roar shattered the heavy stillness. A young, ravenous lion lunged from the shadows, its jaws open to strike. In a terrifying, instantaneous surge of raw, supernatural power, Samson did not run. With nothing but his bare hands, he tore the beast apart as easily as one might rip a fragile piece of cloth.

[secret] Breathing heavily, he wiped the blood from his hands and left the shattered carcass to the sun. When he finally rejoined his parents on the dusty road, he spoke only of the woman he wished to marry, keeping the monstrous violence of the lion—and the terrifying magnitude of his own strength—a dark, unspoken secret.
