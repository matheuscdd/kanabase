Think about the walled cities you are building in your own life right now.

Are those defenses built on a foundation of true integrity and full-hearted service?
Or are they just built on convenience?
Because ancient history proves time and time again that the thickest walls in the world cannot protect a nation or a person if the heart inside those walls becomes empty.
Keep building, but never forget why you are building.
