We want our foundations to be clean.
We always want a clean slate.
Exactly.
But this chapter shows us the exact opposite.
Perhaps the places in your life where you feel you have failed the most,
the places where your pride has caused the most damage
and where you have had to hit the dirt and repent the hardest,
perhaps those are the exact places where the most beautiful,
lasting, and holy foundations are actually built.
The messy reality of our own failures
when they are met with genuine ownership and costly repentance
can become the bedrock for something truly magnificent.
It is a powerful reminder that our worst mistakes do not have to be the end of our story.
Under the mercy of God, they can become the very foundation of a new one.
