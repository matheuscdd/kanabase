They cannot describe the hidden kindness in your heart.
That is tough.
They can only record your physical actions over the past week.
They can only record how you spoke to your family,
how you handled your resources,
and how you treated the strangers who crossed your path.
Without the ability to explain yourself,
What would your character profile actually look like on the page?
Based purely on the hard evidence of your actions,
would the reader see a Gaius taking risks to open doors and share resources?
Or would they see a Diotrephes tightly controlling your environment,
shutting doors, and protecting your own status at the expense of others?
That is the mirror the text holds up to us.
It strips away all our excuses and forces us to realize
that our legacy is being written every single day.
It is not written by what we intend to do, but by how we actively treat the people standing right in front of us.
That's the ultimate takeaway.
We started today analyzing an ancient letter from 95 A.D., but it turns out the letter is actually reading us.
