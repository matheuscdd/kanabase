# Mudanças de pronomes, tempo e lugar no reported speech

## Resumo
No reported speech, além do verbo, muitas vezes mudamos pronomes, expressões de tempo e palavras de lugar para manter o ponto de vista correto.

## Explicação
Se alguém diz `I will come here tomorrow`, isso pode virar `She said that she would go there the next day`. O conteúdo continua o mesmo, mas ajustado ao contexto de quem relata.

No B1, essa adaptação é essencial para que a frase reportada soe natural e lógica.

## Estrutura
- Pronouns change according to speaker and listener
- Time and place expressions may shift according to context

## Exemplos
- She said, "I am tired today." -> She said that she was tired that day.
  Tradução: Ela disse: "Estou cansada hoje." -> Ela disse que estava cansada naquele dia.
- He said, "I will see you tomorrow." -> He said that he would see me the next day.
  Tradução: Ele disse: "Vou ver você amanhã." -> Ele disse que me veria no dia seguinte.
- They said, "We are here now." -> They said that they were there then.
  Tradução: Eles disseram: "Estamos aqui agora." -> Eles disseram que estavam lá naquele momento.
- Ana said, "My brother called me yesterday." -> Ana said that her brother had called her the day before.
  Tradução: Ana disse: "Meu irmão me ligou ontem." -> Ana disse que o irmão dela tinha ligado para ela no dia anterior.
- He told me, "I can help you tonight." -> He told me that he could help me that night.
  Tradução: Ele me disse: "Posso ajudar você hoje à noite." -> Ele me disse que poderia me ajudar naquela noite.

## Observações
- As mudanças dependem do contexto real da fala.
- Nem toda palavra precisa mudar; mudamos o que for necessário para manter sentido e perspectiva.

## Erros comuns
- Errado: She said that I was tired today.
- Certo: She said that she was tired that day.
- Errado: He said he would come here tomorrow.
- Certo: He said he would come there the next day.

## Prática
1. Reescreva: `"I will call you tomorrow," she said to me.`
2. Reescreva: `"We are here now," they said.`
3. Traduza: `Ele disse que viria naquela noite.`

## Gabarito
1. She told me that she would call me the next day.
2. They said that they were there then.
3. He said that he would come that night.
